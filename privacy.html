Privacy Policy

Nourish me Bro · last updated 10 September 2026

Nourish me Bro is a calorie and habit tracker. It is run by one person, not a company. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third party is ever sent your data or paid for it. Nothing you log is sold, shared, or used to train anything.

What is stored

Only what you type in yourself. If you use the app without an account, it never leaves your device. If you create an account, this is synced so it can reach your other devices:

  • Account: your email address, and your name if you enter one.
  • Health and fitness: the food and drink you log, your habits and their streaks, water intake, and the body details you choose to give for the calorie target — age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal. Every one of these is optional and can be left blank.
  • Your own foods: any custom food you add.

No location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifiers, no advertising ID. The app has no access to any of those.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, a managed Postgres host, used purely as storage for this app. Your rows are restricted to your own account by database row-level security, and your password is handled by Supabase's authentication service — it is never visible to this app or its author. Traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. A copy is also kept in your browser's storage so the app works offline.

Deleting your data

In the app, open You → Your data → Erase everything. That wipes the local copy and deletes your synced rows from the server. To remove the login itself, email the address below asking for account deletion and it will be deleted within 30 days, along with anything still attached to it.

Children

Not directed at children under 13, and no account is knowingly created for one.

Changes and contact
